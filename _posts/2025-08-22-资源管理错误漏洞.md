扫出来的漏洞如下：

- diffie hellman·group14-sha1,diffie hellman-group14-sha256,diffie.helman-group16.sha512, diffie hellman- group18-sha512, diffie-hellman-group-exchange-sha256


打印下现在的

```sh
sshd -T | grep kexalgorithms

sudo nano /etc/ssh/sshd_config
```

把不合要求的删掉之后，保存ctrl+O,离开ctrl+X

重启下：

```sh
sudo systemctl reload sshd
```
